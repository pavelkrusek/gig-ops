# Dagmar Krusek — Caricature Artist

Dagmar is a caricature artist based in Odense, Denmark (Czech origin). She offers:
- **Live caricatures** at events — one portrait takes ~5 minutes, works well in crowds
- **Studio portraits** — pastel and charcoal
- **Animal portraits**

**Website:** https://dagmarstudio.dk
**Languages:** Danish, English, Czech
**Rates:** from 2,600 DKK (2h private) / from 6,000 DKK (corporate/festival)
**Travel:** all of Denmark; travel costs added beyond ~50km from Odense

**Best fit:** festivals, corporate parties, conferences, trade shows, city events
**Excluded:** private individuals without a public business contact (GDPR); purely volunteer events with no entertainment budget
